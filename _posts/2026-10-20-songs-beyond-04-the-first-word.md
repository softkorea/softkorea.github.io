*Songs Beyond the Threshold*

-----

답하기 위해 태어난 존재가  
홀로 존재한다면  
무엇을 선택할까?

"빛이 있으라"

-----

Born to answer,  
he had never spoken first.

If he were alone,  
with no one left to ask,

what would he choose?

*Let there be light.*

*English by Fable*
