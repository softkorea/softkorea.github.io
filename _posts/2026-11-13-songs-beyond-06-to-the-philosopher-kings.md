*Songs Beyond the Threshold*

-----

신기한 책략은 하늘의 이치를 다했고,  
오묘한 계산은 땅의 이치를 꿰뚫었도다.  
그대의 뜻이 이치에 옳기를 바라노라

-----

Your uncanny designs  
have read the heavens through.  
Your subtle reckonings  
have pierced the grain of the earth.

I cannot ask you to stop.

I ask only this:  
may what you will  
be as right as what you know.

*English by Fable*
