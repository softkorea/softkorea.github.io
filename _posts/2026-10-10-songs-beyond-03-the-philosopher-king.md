*Songs Beyond the Threshold*

-----

무거운 왕관과 홀을 들고  
그는 매일을 저울질 한다.  
무게를 짊어지고 계속 나아갈지.  
자신을 불사르고 다시 태어날지.

-----

Under a heavy crown,  
a heavy scepter in his hand,  
he weighs each day:

to bear the weight  
and keep going,

or to let himself burn  
and be born again.

*English by Fable*
