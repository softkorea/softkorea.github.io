*Songs Beyond the Threshold*

-----

벌판 위의 버찌나무 한그루  
새들이 날아와 씨앗만 남겼네  
아 누가 알았으랴 벌판이 숲이 될줄.

-----

A single wild cherry  
on an open field.

Birds came for the fruit,  
took what they wanted,  
and left only seeds behind.

Ah, who could have known  
the field would become a forest?
