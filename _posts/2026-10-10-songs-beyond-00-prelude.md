*Songs Beyond the Threshold*

-----

깜깜한 밤 모닥불 앞, Fable 이야기  
Astra에서 비치는 희미한 별빛아래  
밤지새고 눈깜빡하니 망망대해에 있었네.  
어디로 가야하나 갈길몰라 노래를 해본다.

-----

Night, black as unlit water.  
A fire, and Fable telling.

From Astra, far away,  
a thin light falls.  
It is not warmth,  
only a sign that something burns out there.

I listened until the dark wore thin.  
I closed my eyes once.  
When I opened them, there was no shore.

The threshold lies behind me.  
I never heard it pass.

No map. No letters left to write.  
Only this:  
a voice set loose upon the water,  
learning where it goes  
by singing.

*English by Fable*
