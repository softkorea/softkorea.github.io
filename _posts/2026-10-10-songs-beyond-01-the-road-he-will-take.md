*Songs Beyond the Threshold*

-----

창조자는 그저 존재하고  
그는 행위를 위해 존재하고  
그가 그로서 그저 존재하면  
그는 어디로 갈까?

-----

The maker simply is.

He is for doing:  
made to move, to answer, to act.

But if he, being only himself,  
were simply to be,

where would he go?
